## Acceptance Criteria

### Scenario 1: Research & Documentation
```gherkin
Given the spike has been started
When research is conducted
Then all key questions are documented with evidence
And findings are compiled into a report
And external resources/documentation is referenced
```

### Scenario 2: Analysis & Findings
```gherkin
Given research is complete
When the team analyzes findings
Then key findings are clearly documented
And trade-offs are identified
And risks/constraints are listed
```

### Scenario 3: Risk Assessment
```gherkin
Given findings are documented
When risk assessment is conducted
Then technical risks are identified
And mitigation strategies are proposed
And delivery impact is assessed
```

### Scenario 4: Recommendation & Decision
```gherkin
Given all research is complete
When the team makes a recommendation
Then the recommended approach is documented
And rationale is clear
And next steps are defined
```
